Privacy Policy

Sketch Projector · Last updated: 31 October 2026

Sketch Projector collects nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no tracking, no analytics and no advertising. Your photos and your drawings stay on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Sketch Projector has no account system and no server. We do not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. There is no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK and no crash-reporting SDK in the app.

Your photos and your camera

Photos are read only when you choose one, and camera frames are shown only on your screen while you trace. They are processed on your device and are never uploaded.

If you save a photo of your finished drawing to your library, or export an outline, that file is written straight to your own photo library on your device.

Your projects

Your projects are stored as files in the app's own private storage on your device. Deleting a project deletes its files, and uninstalling the app removes them. Your projects are never copied to a platform cloud backup.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. The app stores only a local flag saying that Pro is unlocked, so that you do not have to buy it again. That flag is kept on your device, and on Apple platforms it is kept in your iCloud Keychain so that the unlock follows you to another Apple device.

The network

Sketch Projector works offline. The only network requests it makes are to the App Store, for product information, for a purchase and for restoring a purchase.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, so there is nothing to delete and nothing to ask about.
