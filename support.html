Support

Sketch Projector · a photo to outline tracing app

Something not working? Tell us what happened and we will help. The app has a "Show details" button under most error messages — including what it says is usually all we need.

Get in touch

Email: mhqurashi.apps+support@gmail.com

It helps to know

  • Your iPhone model and the iOS version.
  • Which part you were on: the photo picker, the outline editor, the tracing screen, the print screen or the settings.
  • What you expected to happen, and what happened instead.
  • A screenshot, if you can take one.
  • The text under "Show details", if there was one.

A few things worth knowing

  • The app needs the camera only while you trace. The photo library is only read when you pick a picture.
  • Your projects are on your device. If you reinstall the app they are gone, so keep the drawings you care about.
  • The Pro unlock is bought once and restored from the store, so it follows you to a new phone without buying it again.
  • Everything is processed on your device, so nothing is ever waiting on a server.
